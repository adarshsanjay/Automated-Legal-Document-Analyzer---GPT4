The Client shall provide requirements and review each milestone within ten business days.\n\nPayment: The Client shall pay INR 300,000. Thirty percent is due on signing, forty percent on beta delivery, and thirty percent on final acceptance. Invoices are payable within fifteen calendar days.\n\nConfidentiality: Each party shall protect the other party's confidential information. These obligations continue for three years after termination.\n\nIntellectual property: Upon full payment, the Provider assigns project-specific source code to the Client.\n\nTerm and termination: This agreement starts on 1 March 2026 and continues for twelve months unless terminated earlier. Either party may terminate for material breach if not remedied within thirty days after written notice. Either party may terminate for convenience with sixty days' written notice.\n
